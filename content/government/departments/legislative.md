# The Sangguniang Panlungsod

The **Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council)** is the legislative body of the **Local Government Unit of Puerto Princesa City**. It enacts city ordinances, adopts resolutions, and approves the annual budget and development plans of the city.

---

# Composition (2025–2028 Term)

The City Council is composed of the **Vice Mayor** (presiding officer), **10 elected councilors**, and **ex-officio members** representing the Liga ng mga Barangay and the Sangguniang Kabataan Federation.

# Get Sanitary Permit and City Health Card (Green Card) — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Health Office (CHO)** issues the official **Sanitary Permit** for commercial establishments and the **City Health Certificate (Green Card)** for all food handlers, salon workers, and service industry employees in Puerto Princesa City.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Health Office (CHO) Sanitation Division, City Government Center, Barangay Irawan          |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Laboratory sample collection: 8:00 AM – 10:30 AM)         |
| **Validity**        | Health Cards and Sanitary Permits are valid for **one (1) calendar year**                      |
| **Mandatory For**   | Food establishment owners, cooks, waiters, market stall vendors, hotel staff, salons, and spas |
| **Processing Time** | 1 to 2 working days upon completion of required laboratory exams                               |

---

## Requirements

### For Individual Health Card (Green Card)

- Duly accomplished Health Certificate Application Form.
- 1x1 ID pictures (2 copies) with white background.
- Routine Stool Examination (Fecalysis) result from an accredited government or licensed laboratory.
- Chest X-Ray result (valid within the last 6 months).
- Urinalysis result.
- Attendance Certificate from the Food Safety and Sanitation Orientation Seminar conducted by CHO.

### For Business Establishment Sanitary Permit

- Copy of Mayor's Business Permit / BPLO Assessment.
- Health Cards of all employed workers and food handlers.
- Potability / Microbiological Water Test analysis (for restaurants, water refilling stations, and resorts).
- Sanitary Inspection Fee official receipt from the City Treasurer's Office.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Laboratory Screening:** Undergo required medical diagnostic tests (chest X-ray, fecalysis, urinalysis) at the City Health Laboratory or an accredited medical diagnostic center.
2. **Food Safety Orientation:** Attend the food handlers and sanitary hygiene seminar held weekly by the CHO Sanitation Inspection team.
3. **Medical Evaluation:** Present laboratory results to the City Health Officer / Medical Officer for clinical physical assessment.
4. **Payment:** Settle the laboratory fees, health certificate fee, and sanitary sticker fees at the City Treasurer's cashier.
5. **Issuance:** Have your picture and signature encoded; claim your official laminated **City Health Card (Green Card)**.

# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects — Puerto Princesa City

Socialized housing programs for low-income and informal settler families are coordinated between the **City Government** (through CSWDO and the city's housing/urban development functions) and national shelter agencies like the **National Housing Authority (NHA)**.

---

## 1) Who Typically Qualifies

- Filipino citizens, residents of Puerto Princesa City
- Homeless or informal settler families, or those displaced by government infrastructure projects
- Household income within the socialized housing threshold set under the **Urban Development and Housing Act (RA 7279)**
- No other real property owned

---

## 2) What's Involved

- **Socialized housing projects** — government-assisted housing developments for qualified beneficiaries, often requiring a beneficiary listing/validation process
- **On-site or off-site relocation** — for families affected by government projects (roads, flood control, danger zones)

---

## How to Apply

1. Inquire at the **CSWDO** or the city office handling housing/urban development about current socialized housing programs and beneficiary listing.
2. Prepare requirements: valid ID, Barangay Certificate of Residency/Indigency, proof of income, and certification of no other real property (as required).
3. Attend validation interviews or community assemblies, if part of the program's process.
4. Wait for beneficiary selection results — socialized housing programs typically have limited slots relative to demand.

# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition — Puerto Princesa City

Families facing demolition or eviction have rights and protections under the **Urban Development and Housing Act (RA 7279)**, which requires adequate relocation for qualified informal settler families before eviction or demolition.

---

## Your Rights Before a Demolition

Under RA 7279, government-initiated demolitions of underprivileged and homeless citizens generally require:

- **Adequate notice** (at least 30 days) before the actual demolition or eviction
- **Consultations** with affected families on the demolition and resettlement timetable
- **Presence of local government officials** during the demolition
- **Adequate relocation**, whether temporary or permanent, except in specific cases (e.g., danger areas, government infrastructure projects with a resettlement plan)

---

## How to Ask About Relocation Sites

1. If you receive a **notice of demolition**, contact your **Barangay Hall** and the **CSWDO** immediately to ask about the relocation plan for your case.
2. Attend any scheduled **consultation or dialogue** — this is your opportunity to raise concerns and confirm relocation details.
3. Ask for the demolition/relocation process to be explained in writing, including the timetable and the relocation site being offered.

---

## If You Believe Your Rights Are Being Violated

- Document everything: notices received, dates, and any communication from the demolition team.
- Raise concerns with the **CSWDO** or seek assistance from the **Public Attorney's Office (PAO)** for legal guidance.

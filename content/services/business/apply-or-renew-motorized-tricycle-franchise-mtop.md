# Apply or Renew Motorized Tricycle Franchise (MTOP) — Puerto Princesa City

The **Motorized Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Board (MTFRB)**, in coordination with the **City Traffic Management Office (CTMO)**, regulates the operation of public tricycles within Puerto Princesa City.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | MTFRB Secretariat Office / City Traffic Management Office (CTMO), City Government Center |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                                      |
| **Renewal Period**  | Annual renewal based on the last digit of the designated tricycle body number            |
| **Document Issued** | Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) & Official Fare Taripa Sticker               |
| **Coverage**        | Authorized routes and designated zoning clusters in Puerto Princesa                      |

---

## Requirements

### For Franchise Renewal (Annual)

- Previous year's MTOP Permit and Official Receipts (OR/CR of the motorcycle).
- Valid LTO Certificate of Registration (CR) and Official Receipt (OR) in the applicant's name.
- Valid Professional Driver's License of the designated driver with tricycle code.
- Barangay Clearance of the operator and driver.
- Certificate of Roadworthiness & Motor Vehicle Physical Inspection report from CTMO.
- Comprehensive Third-Party Liability (CTPL) Passenger Insurance policy.
- City Health Card (Green Card) of the driver.

### For New Application or Transfer of Franchise

- All renewal documents listed above.
- Deed of Sale or Transfer of Franchise (notarized), if acquiring an existing slot.
- Voter's Certification or proof of residency in Puerto Princesa City.
- Police Clearance of the driver and operator.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Unit Physical Inspection:** Bring the motorcycle and sidecar to the CTMO Inspection Bay (testing brakes, signal lights, headlamps, muffler decibels, sidecar dimensions, and required body number stencil).
2. **Document Evaluation:** Submit the inspection pass slip and application papers to the MTFRB Secretariat.
3. **Assessment & Payment:** Pay the franchise fee, inspection fee, sticker fee, and regulatory charges at the City Treasurer's Office window.
4. **Approval & Issuance:** Claim your official MTOP, current year franchise sticker, and the laminated Official Fare Taripa matrix. Affix the sticker on the designated sidecar windshield area.

# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries — Puerto Princesa City

The city government manages public markets and public cemeteries in Puerto Princesa. Here's how to apply for a market stall or inquire about cemetery services.

---

## 1) Renting a Public Market Stall

**Typical requirements:**

- Valid ID and Barangay Clearance
- Business Permit / Mayor's Permit application (a stall counts as a business location — see [Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes](/services/business/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes) for the general permitting process)
- Application form for stall rental, submitted to the market administration

**How to apply:**

1. Inquire at the **public market administration office** (on-site at your target market) about available stalls and rental rates.
2. Submit your application along with business permit requirements.
3. Pay the assessed rental fee (rates and terms are set by the city's market operations).

---

## 2) Public Cemetery Services

**What's available:**

- Burial lot application/rental in LGU-managed public cemeteries
- Niche or lot renewal for existing burial spaces

**How to apply:**

1. Visit the **cemetery administration office** or the **City Treasurer's Office** to inquire about available lots and current rates.
2. Bring a valid ID and the death certificate (for a new burial) or your existing lot/niche records (for renewal).
3. Pay the applicable fees; keep your official receipt as proof.

# Settle Traffic Violation or Contest Citation (CTMO) — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Traffic Management Office (CTMO)** enforces city traffic ordinances, regulates vehicular movement, and processes citation tickets issued across Puerto Princesa City.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information            | Details                                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**              | City Traffic Management Office (CTMO) / Traffic Adjudication Board, City Hall Complex, Barangay Irawan |
| **When**               | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                                                    |
| **Payment Deadline**   | Within **seventy-two (72) hours** (3 working days) from ticket issuance to avoid penalty escalation    |
| **Contesting Window**  | File a written notice of contest with the Traffic Adjudication Board within 3 working days             |
| **Impounded Vehicles** | Impoundment Yard located at CTMO compound, Irawan                                                      |

---

## Requirements

### To Pay Traffic Citation Fines

- Original Copy of the Ordinance Violation Receipt (OVR / Traffic Citation Ticket).
- Driver's License or valid government-issued ID.
- Cash or approved payment method for official fine settlement.

### For Claiming Impounded Motor Vehicles / Tricycles

- Original OVR Citation Ticket.
- Original and photocopy of Certificate of Registration (CR) and latest Official Receipt (OR) from LTO.
- Valid Driver's License of the owner or authorized driver.
- Notarized Special Power of Attorney (SPA) if representative is claiming on behalf of the registered owner.
- Official Receipt of settled fines and daily impoundment storage fee.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Verification:** Present your citation ticket at the CTMO Records and Assessment Window.
2. **Assessment:** The traffic clerk assesses the violation based on the City Traffic Code (e.g., illegal parking, no helmet, disregarding traffic officer/sign, overloading, unregistered vehicle).
3. **Contesting (Optional):** If you believe the apprehension was invalid or erroneous, request a referral to the **Traffic Adjudication Board** for an immediate hearing with the apprehending officer's report.
4. **Payment:** Settle the assessed fine at the City Treasurer's Office cashier; obtain an Official Receipt (OR).
5. **Release:** Present the Official Receipt to CTMO to retrieve confiscated driver's licenses or obtain a vehicle release gate pass.

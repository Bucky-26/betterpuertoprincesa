# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage — Puerto Princesa City

Damaged city roads, bridges, and drainage systems can be reported to the **City Engineer's Office** or your **Barangay Hall**, which coordinates repairs with the city government or, for national roads, the **DPWH Palawan 3rd District Engineering Office**.

---

## 1) What to Report

- Potholes, cracked or collapsed road sections
- Damaged or unsafe bridges
- Clogged or damaged drainage causing flooding
- Broken streetlights and road signage

---

## 2) How to Report

1. **Barangay Level (First Step)** — report to your Barangay Hall, especially for barangay roads and local drainage.
2. **City Engineer's Office** — for city roads, bridges, and drainage infrastructure.
3. **DPWH Palawan 3rd District Engineering Office** — for national roads and DPWH-implemented flood control/drainage projects (see current infrastructure projects on this portal's [Statistics page](/statistics)).

**When reporting, include:**

- Exact location (street name, nearest barangay/landmark)
- Description and, if possible, photos of the damage
- Whether the damage poses an immediate safety hazard

---

## 3) Immediate Safety Hazards

If damage poses an immediate danger (e.g., a large pothole causing accidents, a collapsing structure), also notify the **CDRRMO** or call **911**.

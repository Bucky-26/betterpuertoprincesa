# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** processes ID applications and assistance for senior citizens, solo parents, and persons with disabilities (PWDs) under national laws implemented locally.

---

## 1) Senior Citizen ID & Benefits (RA 9994)

**Who qualifies:** Filipino citizens 60 years old and above, residing in Puerto Princesa City.

**Benefits:** 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicine, medical/dental services, transportation, restaurants, and recreation centers; social pension for indigent seniors (per DSWD guidelines); death benefit assistance.

**Requirements:**

- PSA Birth Certificate or any valid government-issued ID showing birthdate
- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- 2 recent 1x1 photos

**Where to apply:** CSWDO or your **Barangay Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)** desk.

---

## 2) Solo Parent ID & Benefits (RA 8972 / RA 11861 - Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act)

**Who qualifies:** Individuals raising a child or children alone due to death of spouse, separation, abandonment, or other qualifying circumstances under the law.

**Benefits:** 10% discount on baby's milk, food, medicine; flexible work schedule (for employed solo parents); parental leave benefits; livelihood and educational assistance where available.

**Requirements:**

- Barangay Certificate of Residency and Solo Parent status
- Birth certificate(s) of child/children
- Proof of solo parent status (death certificate, court order, or affidavit, depending on circumstance)
- 1x1 photo

**Where to apply:** CSWDO.

---

## 3) PWD ID & Benefits (RA 10754)

**Who qualifies:** Persons with apparent or non-apparent disability, residing in Puerto Princesa City.

**Benefits:** 20% discount and VAT exemption similar to senior citizens; PhilHealth premium assistance for indigent PWDs; educational and livelihood assistance where available.

**Requirements:**

- Medical certificate from a licensed physician confirming the disability (or school/employer certification, depending on disability type)
- Barangay Certificate of Residency
- 1x1 photo

**Where to apply:** CSWDO.

---

## How to Apply (General Steps)

1. Secure a **Barangay Certificate** confirming residency and status (senior/solo parent/PWD).
2. Bring all requirements to the **CSWDO** (Ground Floor, Old City Hall Building, Barangay Sta. Monica).
3. Fill out the application form; submit for evaluation.
4. Claim your ID once approved (processing time varies by office volume).

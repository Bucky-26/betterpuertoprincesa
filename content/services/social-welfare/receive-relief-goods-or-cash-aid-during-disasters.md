# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters — Puerto Princesa City

During typhoons, floods, and other emergencies, relief distribution and cash assistance are coordinated by the **CSWDO**, **CDRRMO**, and barangay disaster committees, often with DSWD support.

---

## 1) Relief Goods Distribution

**What it is:** Food packs and basic necessities distributed to affected families during and after a declared disaster or calamity.

**How it works:**

1. Barangay officials conduct a **damage assessment and needs validation** after the disaster.
2. Affected households are listed by the barangay and endorsed to CSWDO/CDRRMO.
3. Relief goods are distributed at the **barangay hall** or a designated evacuation/distribution center.

**What to bring:** Valid ID and proof of residency in the affected barangay.

---

## 2) Cash Assistance (AICS / DSWD-Coordinated)

**What it is:** The **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)** program provides cash or in-kind assistance to individuals and families in crisis, including disaster-affected residents, coordinated locally by CSWDO with DSWD Field Office MIMAROPA.

**Requirements (typical):**

- Valid ID
- Barangay Certificate of Residency/Indigency
- Proof of the crisis situation (e.g., certification from barangay/CDRRMO for disaster-related claims)

**Where to apply:** CSWDO, or through your barangay for disaster-specific payouts announced after a calamity.

---

## 3) Evacuation Assistance

- During a declared emergency, barangays and CDRRMO coordinate **evacuation to designated centers** (schools, barangay halls, covered courts).
- Bring an emergency kit if possible: ID, cash, medicines, water, and a change of clothes.
- Follow official announcements from the **City Information Office** and your barangay for evacuation orders and "all clear" notices.

---

## How to Avail (Quick Steps)

1. **Register with your barangay** as an affected household after a disaster (barangay conducts the assessment).
2. **Wait for the distribution schedule** announced by the barangay or CDRRMO.
3. **Bring a valid ID** and claim relief goods or cash assistance at the designated site.
4. For crisis cash assistance outside a declared disaster, **apply directly at CSWDO**.

# Get Family and Community Welfare Support — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** provides case management, counseling, and referral services for families and individuals facing welfare concerns.

---

## 1) Case Management & Counseling

**What it is:** Social workers assess a family or individual's situation (e.g., domestic issues, child welfare concerns, abuse or neglect cases) and provide counseling, intervention, or referral to appropriate services.

**How to access:** Walk in at CSWDO, or ask your barangay to endorse your case if you're unable to visit directly.

---

## 2) Child Welfare Services

- **Protective custody and intervention** for children in especially difficult circumstances (abuse, neglect, abandonment), coordinated with DSWD and, when necessary, the Philippine National Police Women and Children Protection Desk.
- **Foster care and adoption processing assistance** — CSWDO can guide families through the national process administered by DSWD.

---

## 3) Violence Against Women and Children (VAWC) Assistance

- Report VAWC cases at your **Barangay VAWC Desk** or directly to CSWDO.
- CSWDO coordinates with the **PNP Women and Children Protection Desk** and can assist with protection orders, shelter referrals, and case documentation.

---

## 4) Certificates & Documentation

- **Certificate of Indigency** — for residents needing proof of low income for hospital, school, or legal fee waivers.
- **Social Case Study Report** — prepared by a CSWDO social worker for court cases, medical assistance applications, or other formal requirements.

**Requirements (typical):** Valid ID, Barangay Certificate of Residency, and a brief explanation of the purpose of the request.

---

## How to Avail (Quick Steps)

1. Visit **CSWDO** (Ground Floor, Old City Hall Building, Barangay Sta. Monica) or ask your barangay to refer your case.
2. Explain your concern to the social worker on duty.
3. Provide requested documents (valid ID, Barangay Certificate, etc.).
4. Follow up as advised — some services (case studies, referrals) take a few days to process.

# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs — Puerto Princesa City

Livelihood and skills training for residents are coordinated by the **CSWDO**, the **City PESO**, and partner national agencies (TESDA, DTI, DOLE).

---

## 1) Skills Training (TESDA-Accredited)

**What it is:** Technical-vocational training (e.g., cooking, dressmaking, computer literacy, driving, electrical work) delivered through TESDA-accredited programs, often in partnership with the city.

**How to join:**

1. Watch for **training announcements** from CSWDO, City PESO, or your barangay.
2. Register with basic requirements: valid ID, Barangay Certificate, and (for some courses) proof of unemployment or indigency.
3. Attend the full training course; a **National Certificate (NC)** may be issued upon completion and assessment.

---

## 2) Livelihood Assistance & Starter Kits

**What it is:** Seed capital, tools, or starter kits (e.g., sewing machines, cooking equipment, small trading capital) provided to qualified beneficiaries — often indigent families, solo parents, PWDs, or disaster-affected residents — to start a small livelihood.

**How to apply:**

1. Inquire at **CSWDO** about current livelihood assistance programs and eligibility.
2. Submit requirements: valid ID, Barangay Certificate of Residency/Indigency, and a simple livelihood plan (if required).
3. Attend an orientation session if the program requires one.

---

## 3) Employment & Job Fairs (City PESO)

**What it is:** The **City Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** connects residents to job openings, job fairs, and the government's **Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)**.

**How to join:**

1. Follow **City PESO of Puerto Princesa** on Facebook for job fair schedules and openings.
2. Register at the PESO office (Room 202, 2nd Floor, Old City Hall Building) with a resume and valid ID.

# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings — Puerto Princesa City

The **Office of the City Agriculturist** distributes free agricultural and fishery inputs to registered farmers and fisherfolk, often in partnership with the Department of Agriculture (DA) and the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR).

---

## 1) What's Typically Available

- **Seeds and seedlings** — vegetable seeds, fruit tree seedlings, and rice/corn seeds (seasonal, subject to program availability)
- **Fertilizers** — organic or inorganic fertilizer subsidies, often tied to national DA programs
- **Fingerlings** — tilapia or other fingerlings for backyard or community fish farming, coordinated with BFAR

---

## 2) Who Can Avail

- Farmers and fisherfolk registered in the **Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)**
- Residents of Puerto Princesa's agricultural barangays with an active farm or fishing livelihood

**Tip:** If you're not yet registered under RSBSA, ask the City Agriculturist's Office how to register — many input-distribution programs require it.

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit the **Office of the City Agriculturist** or your **Barangay Agricultural Technician**.
2. Confirm current input-distribution schedules — these are seasonal and depend on DA/BFAR allocations.
3. Submit RSBSA registration (if not yet registered) and basic requirements (valid ID, proof of farm/fishing activity).
4. Claim your allocation on the scheduled distribution date.

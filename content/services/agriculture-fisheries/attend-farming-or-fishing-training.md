# Attend Farming or Fishing Training — Puerto Princesa City

The **Office of the City Agriculturist** organizes training programs for farmers and fisherfolk, often in partnership with the Department of Agriculture (DA), BFAR, and TESDA.

---

## 1) Farming Training Topics (Typical)

- Organic farming and composting
- Modern crop production techniques
- Integrated pest management
- Post-harvest handling and value-adding (e.g., food processing)

## 2) Fishing & Aquaculture Training Topics (Typical)

- Sustainable fishing practices and marine conservation
- Fish cage/pond aquaculture management
- Fish processing and value-adding

---

## Who Can Join

- Registered farmers and fisherfolk (RSBSA registration is often required or prioritized)
- Residents of agricultural or coastal barangays engaged in farming or fishing

---

## How to Join

1. Watch for training announcements from the **Office of the City Agriculturist** or your **Barangay Agricultural Technician**.
2. Register with basic requirements: valid ID, Barangay Certificate, and RSBSA registration (if applicable).
3. Attend the full training — some programs issue a certificate of completion, which can help with future assistance applications.

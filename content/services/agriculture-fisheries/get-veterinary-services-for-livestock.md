# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock — Puerto Princesa City

The **Office of the City Agriculturist** (through its Veterinary Services section) provides animal health services for livestock and poultry owners.

---

## 1) Services Offered

- **Vaccination** — for common livestock diseases (e.g., hog cholera, avian flu prevention measures) as scheduled by the office
- **Deworming and basic treatment** for farm animals
- **Animal health consultation** — advice on livestock care, disease prevention, and reporting of sick or dying animals
- **Anti-rabies vaccination for pets** — coordinated with the City Health Office's Animal Bite Treatment Center program

---

## 2) Reporting Animal Disease Outbreaks

If you notice unusual livestock deaths or signs of disease outbreak (e.g., in poultry or hogs), report immediately to the City Veterinary Office or your barangay — early reporting helps contain outbreaks before they spread.

---

## How to Avail

1. Visit the **Office of the City Agriculturist** or contact your **Barangay Agricultural Technician** to inquire about the next vaccination or deworming schedule in your area.
2. Bring your livestock (or arrange a farm visit for larger animals) on the scheduled date.
3. For sick or dying animals, report right away rather than waiting for the next scheduled visit.

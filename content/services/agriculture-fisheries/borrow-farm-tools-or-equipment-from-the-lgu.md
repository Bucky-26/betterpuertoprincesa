# Borrow Farm Tools or Equipment from the LGU — Puerto Princesa City

The **Office of the City Agriculturist** maintains farm equipment (e.g., hand tractors, threshers, water pumps) that registered farmers may request to borrow or schedule for use, subject to availability.

---

## What May Be Available

- Hand tractors and tillers
- Water pumps for irrigation
- Post-harvest equipment (e.g., threshers, dryers), depending on current inventory

> Availability varies — equipment is typically shared across barangays on a scheduled basis, so book ahead of your planting or harvest season.

---

## How to Request

1. Inquire at the **Office of the City Agriculturist** or through your **Barangay Agricultural Technician** about available equipment and the booking process.
2. Provide your RSBSA registration (if applicable) and details of your farm (location, size, crop).
3. Confirm the schedule and any usage guidelines (fuel, operator, return condition).

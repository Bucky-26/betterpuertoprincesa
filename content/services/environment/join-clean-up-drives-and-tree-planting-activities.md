# Join Clean-Up Drives and Tree Planting Activities — Puerto Princesa City

The **Office of the City Environment and Natural Resources Officer (CENRO)**, together with barangays and civic groups, regularly organizes clean-up and greening activities across Puerto Princesa City — a city with an active environmental policy record, including City Ordinance No. 993 (2021) regulating single-use plastics.

---

## 1) Coastal & River Clean-Up Drives

- Community clean-ups along beaches, rivers, and canals, often tied to **International Coastal Cleanup Day** (September) and other environmental observances.
- Organized with barangays, schools, and civic/environmental organizations.

## 2) Tree Planting Activities

- City-led and partner-led tree planting in watershed areas, along riverbanks, and in reforestation sites.
- Often tied to **Arbor Day** and the city's ongoing forest protection programs (Puerto Princesa hosts significant remaining forest cover, including the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River watershed).

---

## How to Join

1. Follow **CENRO** and the **City Information Office** for announcements of upcoming clean-up drives and tree planting events.
2. Check with your **Barangay Hall** — many clean-up activities are organized at the barangay level.
3. Register (if required) and show up on the scheduled date with appropriate clothing and footwear.

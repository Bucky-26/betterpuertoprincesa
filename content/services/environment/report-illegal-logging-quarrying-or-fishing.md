# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing — Puerto Princesa City

Illegal logging, quarrying, and fishing (including dynamite/cyanide fishing) are serious offenses under national environmental and fisheries laws. Report violations to protect Puerto Princesa's forests, watersheds, and marine resources.

---

## 1) What to Report

| Violation             | Examples                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Illegal logging**   | Cutting trees without a permit, especially in forest/watershed areas                     |
| **Illegal quarrying** | Unpermitted extraction of sand, gravel, or stone                                         |
| **Illegal fishing**   | Dynamite fishing, cyanide fishing, fishing in marine protected areas, use of banned gear |

---

## 2) How to Report

1. **Barangay Level (First Step)** — report to your Barangay Hall, especially for local, small-scale violations.
2. **CENRO** — for logging, quarrying, and land-based environmental violations within city jurisdiction.
3. **DENR-Palawan (PENRO)** — for violations involving forest lands and resources under direct DENR jurisdiction.
4. **Bantay Dagat / Philippine Coast Guard / PNP Maritime** — for illegal fishing activities at sea.

**When reporting, provide:**

- Exact location and time of the violation
- Description of what you observed (vehicles, equipment, number of people involved)
- Photos or videos, if it is safe to take them

---

## 3) What Happens After Reporting

- CENRO or DENR inspection teams investigate the report.
- Violators may face fines, confiscation of equipment/catch, and criminal charges under RA 9003 (waste), the Revised Forestry Code, the Philippine Fisheries Code (RA 8550), or the Palawan Council for Sustainable Development's own environmental ordinances (Palawan has special environmental protections under RA 7611, the Strategic Environmental Plan for Palawan Act).

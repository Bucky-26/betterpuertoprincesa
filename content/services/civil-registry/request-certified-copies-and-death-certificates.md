# Request Certified Copies and Death Certificates — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Civil Registrar Office (CCRO)** issues certified copies of vital records (Birth, Marriage, Death) and facilitates burial clearances for the deceased.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information                   | Details                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**                     | City Civil Registrar Office (CCRO), City Government Center, Barangay Irawan |
| **When**                      | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                         |
| **Death Registration Window** | Within 48 hours of death for timely burial permit issuance                  |
| **Certified True Copies**     | Available for all vital events registered within Puerto Princesa City       |
| **Processing Time**           | 30 minutes to 1 day for local certified copies                              |

---

## Requirements

### For Death Certificate Registration & Burial Permit

- Four (4) copies of the Certificate of Death (Municipal Form 103) signed and certified by the attending physician or local health officer.
- Post-Mortem Certificate or Medico-Legal Report (for violent or unattended deaths).
- Transfer of Cadaver Permit (if the deceased will be transported outside Puerto Princesa or Palawan).
- Valid Government ID of the informant/next of kin.

### For Requesting Certified True Copies of Records

- Duly filled Request Slip indicating Registry Number, Name of Document Owner, Date of Event, and Purpose.
- One (1) Valid Government ID of the document owner.
- Authorization Letter and valid ID of the representative (if requested by someone other than the document owner, spouse, parent, or direct descendant).

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Verification:** Inquire at the CCRO Records Section and fill out the request slip. Staff verify whether the document is in the local registry archives.
2. **Assessment & Payment:** Receive the order of payment and settle the document fee per copy at the City Treasurer's Office.
3. **Encoding & Authentication:** Staff retrieves the original registry book, prints the official certified true copy, and prepares the certification seal.
4. **Release:** The City Civil Registrar or assistant signs and seals the document. Claim your official certified true copy with the official receipt attached.

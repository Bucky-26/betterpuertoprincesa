# Register a Birth Certificate — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Civil Registrar Office (CCRO)** of Puerto Princesa City handles the official recording of all births occurring within the city's jurisdiction.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information              | Details                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**                | City Civil Registrar Office (CCRO), Ground Floor, Executive Building, City Government Center, Barangay Irawan |
| **When**                 | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                                                           |
| **Timely Registration**  | Within thirty (30) days from date of birth — **No fee**                                                       |
| **Delayed Registration** | After thirty (30) days from birth — Subject to posting period & minimal legal fee                             |
| **Processing Time**      | Timely: Same day release; Delayed: 10-day mandatory publication/posting period                                |

---

## Requirements

### For Timely Registration (Within 30 Days of Birth)

- Four (4) copies of the Certificate of Live Birth (COLB / Municipal Form 102), duly accomplished and signed by the attending physician, midwife, or nurse.
- Marriage Certificate of Parents (if married).
- Affidavit of Acknowledgment / Paternity and Affidavit of Use of Surname of the Father (AUSF), if parents are not married.
- Valid Government IDs of the parents and informant.

### For Delayed / Late Registration (Beyond 30 Days)

- Certificate of Live Birth accomplished in four (4) copies.
- Negative Certification of Birth from PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority).
- Baptismal Certificate or Early School Records (Form 137 / Diploma) showing birth date and parentage.
- Joint Affidavit of Two Disinterested Persons certifying the birth details and identity.
- Barangay Certification of residency confirming residence in Puerto Princesa City.
- Valid Government IDs of the applicant and witnesses.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Submission:** Present the accomplished Certificate of Live Birth and supporting documents at the CCRO Receiving Window.
2. **Review & Examination:** The Civil Registry clerk verifies the completeness of entries, signatures, and attachments.
3. **Assessment:** For timely registration, there is no registration fee. For delayed registration, secure an assessment slip and pay at the City Treasurer's Office window.
4. **Subscription / Oath:** Parents sign and swear to affidavits before the City Civil Registrar or authorized administering officer.
5. **Registration & Transmittal:** The birth is assigned an official Local Civil Registry (LCR) registry number. A registered copy is released to the parents, and one copy is transmitted to the PSA for national database inclusion.

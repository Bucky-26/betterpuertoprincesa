# Apply for a Marriage License — Puerto Princesa City

Couples intending to marry where at least one party resides in Puerto Princesa City must apply for an official Marriage License at the **City Civil Registrar Office (CCRO)**.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information           | Details                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**             | City Civil Registrar Office (CCRO), City Government Center, Barangay Irawan  |
| **When**              | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                          |
| **Validity**          | Valid anywhere in the Philippines for **120 days** from date of issue        |
| **Mandatory Posting** | Ten (10) consecutive days of public bulletin posting before license issuance |
| **Legal Ages**        | 18–21 (Parental Consent required); 22–25 (Parental Advice required)          |

---

## Requirements

### Basic Requirements for Both Applicants

- PSA Certified True Copy of Birth Certificate (with official receipt).
- Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR) issued by PSA within the last 6 months.
- Barangay Certificate of Residency in Puerto Princesa City.
- Valid Government-issued IDs of both parties showing current address.
- 2x2 ID photos with white background (2 copies each).
- Pre-Marriage Counseling (PMC) and Family Planning Certificate from the **City Health Office (CHO)** and **CSWDO**.

### Additional Requirements Depending on Circumstance

- **Ages 18–21:** Notarized Affidavit of Parental Consent executed by parents.
- **Ages 22–25:** Notarized Affidavit of Parental Advice.
- **Widowed Applicant:** PSA Certified Death Certificate of deceased spouse.
- **Foreign Citizens:** Certificate of Legal Capacity to Contract Marriage issued by their respective embassy in the Philippines, plus passport bio-page and entry stamp.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Attend Pre-Marriage Seminar:** Schedule and complete the required Pre-Marriage Counseling and Responsible Parenthood Seminar at the City Health Office / CSWDO.
2. **File Application:** Submit the accomplished Application for Marriage License and all required documents at the CCRO Marriage Section.
3. **Payment:** Pay the filing fee, license fee, and application fee at the City Treasurer's Office cashier window.
4. **Mandatory 10-Day Posting:** The notice of application is posted on the City Hall bulletin board for ten (10) consecutive calendar days as required by the Family Code.
5. **License Release:** Return on the 11th day to claim your official Marriage License.
